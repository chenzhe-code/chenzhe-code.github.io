abstract: >-
  We introduce SimPCSS, a plug-and-play supervised learning scheme that leverages high-quality simulated point clouds to improve semantic segmentation of sparse and noisy real-world point clouds. Specifically, SimPCSS extracts multi-scale, high-confidence features from labeled simulated data as prior guidance and transfers this knowledge to low-quality point clouds through imitation learning. The method is model-agnostic and consistently improves MinkUNet and PTv3 on both synthetic and real-world datasets.
